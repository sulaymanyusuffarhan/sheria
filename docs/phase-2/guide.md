# Phase 2 — workflow guide

The demonstration is fictional. Never upload TBC files, personal data, credentials or signed counterparts into a demo or unapproved environment.

## Try an existing example

Sign in as **Legal dashboard** and open **Contract register → Audio archive transcription pilot**. Its Parts I–XI are saved and Part XII has a frozen recommendation awaiting a Head decision. Use **View review** to inspect the submitted version, open each checklist section, and download an internal PDF. The accompanying staff request is accessible from the case or from **Notifications**. The **Regional training partnership** example is partially completed and editable by **Legal Officer**. As **Staff portal**, **Archive excerpt for staff training** demonstrates an issued response and an explicitly released document. The ordinary Phase 1 synthetic rows are historical read views and do not have invented Phase 2 approvals.

## Walk through a new request

| Role | What to do and what changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Staff portal** | Choose **New legal request**. The account supplies the name and organisational unit. Enter the background, contact, requested date and priority. For a contract or MoU, enter the counterparty, type, amount/currency (zero where appropriate), duration and attach the draft. Confirm the declaration and submit. Keep the request reference. |
| **Senior Legal Officer** | Find the request in **Notifications** or the **Matter register**. Start preliminary review. For missing details, send a staff-facing question and due date; the requester responds in their portal, then Legal Services verifies the response. Accept once evidence is complete (or reject with a distinct staff-facing disposition). Accepting a contract/MoU files its first version in the contract register. Assign an eligible active legal officer with priority and a deadline. |
| **Legal Officer** | Open the assignment notice, begin legal review and upload any private evidence or proposed deliverables. For a new draft, register a new version; this creates a blank checklist for that version. In the checklist, Parts I–X use explicit **Yes**, **No** and **N/A**. A blank is unanswered and a **No** or **N/A** needs a reason before review. Save each part; enter the risk classification and assessment in XI. In XII, recommend an outcome, write the proposed staff response, and choose any proposed files for release. Submitting creates a frozen, attributed review and notifies the Head. |
| **Head of Legal Services** | Open the frozen package from the case. Check the exact draft, evidence, risk and intended release. Record the decision as a different person from the requester and reviewer. In a non-demo test, use a **fresh TOTP code** (not the sign-in code). In the sandbox, the checkbox records a simulated confirmation only. **Return** or **Conditional** sends work back without clearing it; **Reject** publishes only the separate disposition. Only a legally acceptable recommendation can be cleared. Strategic/Board-Level risk also requires escalation evidence pinned in the package. |
| **Head of Legal Services → Staff portal** | After clearance, choose **Issue approved response** to publish the exact approved text and selected files. The requester receives a notice and sees the published response in the timeline. A completed request can be closed without deleting or overwriting its recorded decision. |

Part XIII is not a pre-clearance condition. If externally signed under applicable authority, a Head can separately record the execution, matching counterpart, dates, and the authority evidence references. Part XIII then becomes editable by the authorised legal officer or Head; it is separately finalised with all six answers, manager, monitoring and archived original details. An expiry date, when supplied, becomes a calendar milestone. Contract text changes require a new version and review; prior snapshots and issued responses remain historical.

## Document and operational rules

- Allowed intake formats: PDF, DOCX and XLSX (5 MiB per file, at most 30 documents per request). Encrypted/unsupported active formats are rejected. The media type is derived by the server, never trusted from the upload. Each file is stored in an encrypted DB blob with a server-generated identifier and SHA-256 hash, not in a public media directory. Downloads recheck identity, current scope, scan status, storage integrity and intended audience.
- Set `LSMS_DOCUMENT_KEY` to a **dedicated Fernet key**, distinct from the TOTP key. Keep it out of Git and logs; retain securely with DB backups. For disposable, synthetic environments only, the README shows one way to generate it. An invalid or missing key prevents upload. Wrong or lost keys block downloads and any approval/release needing the file.
- For a non-demo test, configure a private ClamAV daemon UNIX socket via `LSMS_CLAMD_SOCKET` (INSTREAM). With no accepted scan verdict, uploads remain quarantined. Legal Services can recheck an eligible pending document when the scanner is back; a blocked document is not served. New versions and signed counterparts require an available verdict at upload. Demo/test fallback is a **format check only**, never malware-scan evidence. Validate the real scanner, its resource limits, incident handling, supported formats and recovery before any pilot. Format validation is not content disarm or a substitute for endpoint protections.
- Notifications are in-app only. Wording is redacted; links and counts recheck current scope. There is no email/SMS integration or scheduled deadline/escalation delivery in this build.
- The existing versioned API remains a read API. Workflow forms are CSRF-protected, scoped server commands; there is no generic write API for editing case status. Editing a stale form returns a conflict rather than overwriting the newer version.
