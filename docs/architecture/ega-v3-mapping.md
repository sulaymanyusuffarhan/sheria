## 4. Evidence and approval process

1. **Before build:** TBC appoints owners and reviewers; register applicability and current versions, identify dependencies, complete the alternatives and feasibility review, and route the concept/project and new-system approval work through the applicable ICT/Steering Committee and e-GA process. Record the decision before treating this design as a baseline.
2. **At each phase gate:** Maintain a controlled register of clause → requirement → control → test → evidence → reviewer/date/decision, plus open gaps, mitigations and authorised exceptions. Bind evidence to release, environment, configuration and policy version. Do not store case evidence or privacy-sensitive logs in the source repository or attach them to generic reports.
3. **Before rollout:** Submit the assessed release and hosting evidence through the approved channel, including independent security evidence, UAT/SAT, restore and rollback results and sign-offs. Resolve blocking gaps; obtain required authorisations in writing. Do not use an Arena preview as production or sign-off evidence.
4. **In operation:** Keep annual security assessment and institutional self-assessment/reporting, service/incident evidence, disaster recovery reports, code/repository assurance and post-implementation review current. Audit approved changes to the baseline and re-evaluate material changes.
