Confirm the wording and any layout/numbering ambiguities against TBC's controlled PDF before formal baselining. The July 2022 edition was not used. Entries summarise rather than reproduce the standard. Nested activities are named in the corresponding row; §2.2 is labelled guidelines as published, not presumed optional.

**Status:** **Tested** = demonstrated for the stated synthetic scope only; **Partial** = engineering control or draft evidence exists, but only part of the requirement is supported; **Gap** = missing or conflicting in the reviewed implementation/process; **Unverified** = applicable evidence not supplied or test not run; **Conditional** = applicability or future trigger to be confirmed in writing, not a waiver or a pass; **Reference** = regulator's framework/context rather than an application control. **E01–E10** are evidence IDs, not test counts. Institutional artefacts not supplied may exist outside this review; absence here is not a finding that TBC never performed the work. The **G0, G1, G2, G3** gates are defined in the [report](ega-v3-gap-report.md#priority-findings-and-closure-evidence). All owners below are proposed roles. No row gives institutional or regulatory acceptance.
