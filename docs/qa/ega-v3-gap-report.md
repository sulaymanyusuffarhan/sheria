# TBC LSMS — QA and security gap report

**18 November 2026 · Review of Phases 1–4 · No-go for production and real data**

The checked-in workflows are ready for **further isolated, synthetic testing**, not institutional rollout. The fresh regression suites, browser walkthroughs and a small PostgreSQL restore passed. They do not establish production readiness: production sign-in is intentionally disabled, the dependency scan returned advisories, the source repository is public and `main` is unprotected, and no approved hosting, formal security assessment, signed UAT or signed SAT was supplied. **No production deployment or configuration change was made. This report is not a compliance certification or an e-GA approval.**

## Priority findings and closure evidence

**G0** — baseline scope, custody and requirements decisions; start now. **G1** — before connection to TBC's network or access to institutional integrations; testing beforehand is isolated and synthetic. **G2** — before go-live or any real records. **G3** — recurring operations, with procedures and owners established by G2. These are proposed gates, not delegated approval authority. A date or an owner listed here does not imply acceptance.

## Acceptance and release decisions still required

1. **Baseline before accepting tests.** DLS, Records and ICT sign the BRD/SRS, role/delegation matrix, controlled bilingual checklist and official-source inventory. Agree data migration and reconciliation, reporting periods and suppression, inclusion needs, performance thresholds and RPO/RTO. Record applicability decisions for any shared service, SaaS, supplier or outsourced operating arrangement, and assign the applicable regulatory review route.
2. **UAT — DLS and representative users.** Run end-to-end intake/return/reply/issue, version-specific contract clearance versus separately recorded execution, litigation calendar/settlement, opinion approval/issue, rights and compliance deadlines, sealed documents/holds and reporting. Include staff, departmental leads, legal officers, Head, Records, Management and accessibility users as appropriate. Test withheld access, delegation and actual office hand-offs. The **UAT Report** must record the requirement ID, version/environment, scenario, expected/actual result, evidence pointer, defects and retests, decision, user representative and signature. Automated role sessions are not user signatures.
3. **SAT — ICT, QA and control owners.** With G1 controls in place, verify agreed integrations and target infrastructure, migration reconciliation, SSO and revocation, scanner/key custody, DB least privilege and logging, accepted security results, load/availability, backup/restore and rollback. The **SAT Report** must identify the exact candidate/configuration, acceptance criteria, evidence, remaining risks/duly authorised exceptions, decision and independent technical sign-off. CI jobs and a local restore do not constitute SAT.
4. **Deployment and rollback — approved change only.** Approve the host, recovery site, support staffing and security arrangements first. Sign off the artifact digest, config and migrations, maintenance window/traffic plan, backup checkpoint with keys and rollback decision owner. Run migration and least-privilege checks in staging; smoke-test identity, document read/write, audit, reports and scheduler. Predefine abort criteria. On failure, stop writes as agreed; restore the prior artifact when the schema remains compatible, otherwise invoke the tested data recovery/forward-fix plan. Reconcile post-checkpoint transactions, review document hashes/audit continuity and notify users before reopening. Do not run reverse migrations blindly against live records.
5. **Go-live authority.** TBC ICT and relevant authorities must approve hosting and security arrangements in writing. Obtain the required DLS, ICT, management and regulatory acceptance/assessment records and resolve blockers before any production release or real-data use. No such decision was supplied for this review.
