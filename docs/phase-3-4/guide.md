# Phase 3–4 operator walkthrough

The specialist registers share the matter ID, department, classification, current assignment, authentication and audit trail used by Phase 2. Use these workflows with synthetic data until the institutional gates are closed.

## Register and assign

- **Staff request:** Submit the appropriate category from the staff portal. The scoped Senior or Head verifies intake and accepts it. Open the linked register from the legal matter and choose **Register specialist details**. No second request is created.
- **Legal-originated matter:** A scoped Senior or Head can choose **Open internal matter**, record the classification, and assign an eligible officer on the shared matter. No staff projection is generated. A protected (confidential or restricted) matter is not visible even to the creator after registration unless that person has current named clearance. Provision named clearance through the separately authorised DLS custody procedure; this release does not provide an in-app clearance-granting console. Do not use a less protective classification to get around this gate.
- **Legacy rows:** Phase 1 example/index rows without a workflow remain viewable under the old scopes. The system does not invent intake, assignments, specialist history or decisions for them. Use a controlled import/reconciliation to adopt institutional history.

| Workspace | Walkthrough and controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Litigation register** | Record court/registry, file number when known, type, advocates and parties. Add dated proceedings (filing, court order, judgment, appeal, progress or custody) and exact supporting files, and record costs in their original currency. Strategy notes are shown only to the currently assigned officer and scoped Head. Keep strategy files in a **sealed** repository item, not in ordinary evidence. Record an externally documented outcome only after scheduled hearings are disposed. An external settlement needs separate authority evidence plus the Head’s fresh confirmation, not merely a document titled “settlement”. Closure records the review of appeals, obligations and custody; it is not permission to delete evidence. |
| **Court calendar** | Schedule a hearing from its docket in EAT. Adjourn and reschedule to add a successor rather than overwrite the original. The next hearing and agenda update from the new schedule; **Include hearing history** shows adjourned, cancelled and held rows. The calendar carries the purpose and location, not private strategy; only legal users within current case scope see a row. **Other deadlines** lists active contract, rights and compliance dates. |
| **Legal opinions** | The assigned officer saves a version with the question, verified facts, authorities, analysis and conclusion. A different, department-scoped Senior endorses it or returns it. The author then freezes the endorsed text and evidence from **Send to Head of Legal Services** on the shared matter. A distinct Head uses the existing fresh-authentication decision. Returning an opinion needs a new version and another Senior endorsement. Approval does not issue it. **Issue approved response** records release of the exact approved version. The file and text stay legal-only by default. For an eligible internal-classification staff matter, the frozen package may explicitly name the requesting account for full opinion issue; rejection, lack of issue, reclassification or a different account cannot retrieve it. The requester can still receive a separate approved summary. Close completed opinions on the shared matter; the immutable issue record remains retrievable while authorised. |
| **IP & broadcasting rights** | Record a portfolio and each verified term of rights: asset/programme, licensor, category, platform, territory, uses/reuse, exclusivity, start/expiry and provenance. A related **executed** contract version is selectable only from scoped matters in the same department. Record a renewal as a new linked term, not a new expiry on an old row. Incidents are allegations; record response, optional related litigation, and supervisory resolution separately. A link never confers access to the target matter. Recorded dates or scope do not authorise transmission. |
| **Compliance register** | Add the assigned department/officer and obligation deadline. Record an authoritative HTTPS reference, citation, jurisdiction and effective date. A different scoped supervisor independently verifies the reference. Assess against the current effective verified source. A demonstrated result needs evidence; a gap needs an owner, plan and corrective deadline. Submit exact corrective evidence, then have someone else verify or return it. A source update keeps past assessments and open corrections but requires fresh verification and reassessment. Synthetic control examples are not law or institutional findings. |
| **Document repository** | Open **Documents** on a specialist record or **Document repository** on a shared matter. Search existing evidence or upload a versioned item with title, category, tags, provenance and a retention review date, if applicable. Items inherit matter scope; the **Sealed** option narrows it to the scoped Head and actively assigned officer. Only unsealed, clean, same-matter repository versions can be linked in litigation, rights and compliance records; use ordinary scoped matter evidence for opinion packages. Records personnel see only authorised departmental metadata and can place a hold or archive, not see titles, file names, sources, hashes or bytes. Only a scoped Head can lift a hold. A hold prevents archiving, and unresolved quarantine prevents archiving. Archiving blocks new versions but does not delete existing versions or grant access. |
| **Management overview** | View current/prior calendar-year cohorts, status totals, counts by module/month and operational indicators. Export the disclosed cells as CSV, Excel or PDF. No title, reference, document, reason, party, user or other case detail is exported. Counts are for distinct eligible matters. Small nonzero cells (1–2) and necessary companions are masked; controls apply identically on screen and in exports. Calendar year is the intake cohort, and operational counts reflect the current state as of the report date, not a historical year-end snapshot. |

## Deadline notifications

Case actions emit scoped in-app notices. A separate operator pass delivers deadline reminders for the assigned officer (within seven days, then on the due date) and escalates unresolved hearings and overdue compliance deadlines to the authorised supervisor or Head the day after the date. The worker checks current assignments, named clearance and milestone state, and deduplicates by milestone, recipient, date and window. Adjourned or otherwise resolved work, or loss of scope, removes existing reminders from the visible inbox. The same command can be retried; each delivery and its notification commit together.

```sh
python manage.py deliver_legal_reminders --demo  # synthetic development database only
```

For an approved non-demo environment run without `--demo` under the approved job scheduler. No scheduler, email/SMS gateway, public holiday calculation or external court, broadcasting or regulator feed is configured in the preview. Treat the register and verified court/official source as authoritative; reminders are for follow-up, not service or approval.
